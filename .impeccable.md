## Design Context

### Users
This is the communal office TV, used by coworkers and guests
with the slim solar remote. They need to open installed streaming apps,
connected inputs, casting instructions and a few everyday settings quickly.

### Brand Personality
UTILITY — a tv should be a tool. Use lowercase in the TV UI,
bold editorial type, and direct language. Brutalist structure with minimal
visual noise; retain the existing Heros font and orange focus accent.
Quiet, direct, communal. Copy is compressed and uses everyday-object labels.
The interface is an editorial index for shared office equipment: simple,
room-readable, and free of promotional feeds. Lead with usefulness and
freedom from ads, recommendations, and overstimulation in this interface.

### Aesthetic Direction
One column, bold Helvetica-style bundled Heros, large room-readable type.
White, #2f2f2f and black; #ff7700 only on the focused row. No corner stamps,
decorative badges or orange headline. Auto appearance follows calculated sunrise
and sunset for San Francisco city center, offline; manual light/dark remain.
List rows show the item name and focus arrow only; no secondary descriptions.
AirPlay and screen mirroring are passive footer status, never selectable rows.
Appearance is a single row near the bottom: auto, light or dark, saved locally.
A short lowercase note after the menu explains this shared office TV sidequest. Use we/our or communal language rather than my/mine.
Distinguish detected components from live receiver health; never invent readiness.

### Design Principles
1. Keep one clear remote focus and predictable Back behavior.
2. Show only installed apps and connected external inputs.
3. No settings row: Samsung QuickPanel has unwanted recommendations. Reach full settings through Samsung Home at the bottom. Do not duplicate remote volume/mute controls. On 2026-10-25 the creator explicitly accepted a foreground-only CRT volume OSD as an experiment: current font, orange glow, bars and remaining dots. Keep it transient and out of the menu; global replacement remains an investigation.
4. Preserve the editorial list, generous spacing and focused orange accent.
5. Stay offline, ES5-compatible and inexpensive to render on Tizen 8.
